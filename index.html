Small, family-run, close to the sea

Meth Suites sits on the quiet coastal strip between the Negombo lagoon and the Indian Ocean. With only 10 rooms, the family who runs it gets to know every guest by name.

Arriving on a late flight? Leaving early tomorrow? We're close enough to the airport to make it easy, and far enough away to feel like a holiday.
